# GreenBlu.ai

An AI-powered wellbeing and productivity Chrome extension that helps employees improve their wellbeing through continuous mood monitoring, personalized interventions, and data-driven insights.

## Overview

GreenBlu.ai is inspired by "Chobotničky z druhého poschodia" (a Czechoslovakian TV series) and provides a comprehensive solution for:

- **Individual employees**: Unobtrusive wellbeing support and productivity enhancement
- **Team managers**: Data-driven insights for team optimization and meeting scheduling
- **HR professionals**: Company-wide wellbeing metrics and program effectiveness measurement

## Key Features

- **Mood Tracking**: Science-backed VAD (Valence-Arousal-Dominance) model for emotional state capture
- **Circadian Rhythm Tracking**: Personalized productivity window recommendations based on wake-up time and weather
- **Intervention Modules**: Curated breathing, eye, and physical exercises
- **Behavioral Analysis**: Non-intrusive monitoring of work patterns for stress detection
- **Team Analytics**: Visualization of team energy and optimal collaboration times
- **Personalization**: AI-powered adaptation to individual preferences and patterns
