Manual Steps if buttons don't work:

  1. Open Chrome DevTools: Press F12 or Cmd+Option+I (Mac)
  2. Go to Application tab (top menu)
  3. Clear storage:
    • In left sidebar, click "Storage"
    • Click "Clear site data" button
  4. Disable cache:
    • Go to "Network" tab
    • Check "Disable cache" checkbox at the top
  5. Hard reload: Keep DevTools open, right-click the reload button, select "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"

Or try Incognito Mode:

Open http://localhost:5173/ in an incognito window (Cmd+Shift+N or Ctrl+Shift+N)
